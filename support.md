## 📱 Getting Started

### How do I add a new activity?

1. Open Life Currency.
2. Navigate to the Activities section.
3. Tap the button to create a new activity.
4. Choose a category, duration, and date.
5. Save your activity.

Your activity will be included in your time insights.

### How do I track my progress?

Open the **Progress** section to explore your time patterns.

You can discover how your time is distributed across
different activities and categories.

### What are Weekly Intentions?

Weekly Intentions help you set priorities and dedicate
time to activities that matter to you.

You can create intentions and review your progress
throughout the week.

### What is Life Garden?

Life Garden is a visual representation of your
personal journey and time patterns.

It provides a creative way to reflect on your progress.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data

### Where is my data stored?

Life Currency stores your activities, intentions,
and reflections locally on your device.

### Does Life Currency require an account?

No. You can use Life Currency without creating an account.

### Can I delete my activities?

Yes. You can delete activities directly from the app.

### Can I back up my data?

Life Currency includes local backup functionality.

Make sure to keep your backup files in a safe location.

For more information, read our
[Privacy Policy](./).

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

### The app isn't working correctly. What should I do?

Try the following:

1. Close and reopen Life Currency.
2. Restart your iPhone.
3. Check whether an app update is available.
4. Contact support if the issue continues.

**Important:** Avoid deleting the app before backing up
your data, as locally stored information may be lost.
