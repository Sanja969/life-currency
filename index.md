# 🌌 Life Currency
### Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Life Currency**.

Life Currency is a personal time-awareness and self-reflection application designed to help you understand how you spend your time, recognize meaningful patterns, and live more intentionally.

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Currency handles information you enter into the application.

---

## 2. Information You Provide

Life Currency allows you to create and manage:

- **Daily Activities** — Track how you spend your time.
- **Weekly Intentions** — Set meaningful goals and priorities.
- **Daily Reflections** — Reflect on your experiences.
- **Personal Thoughts** — Save ideas and reflections.
- **Progress Insights** — Explore patterns in your activities.

This information is stored locally on your device.

---

## 3. Data Collection

**Life Currency does not collect personal data from its users.**

Specifically:

- No account registration is required.
- No personal information is transmitted to our servers.
- No activity records or reflections are collected by the developer.
- No information is sold to third parties.
- No advertising trackers are used.

Your personal information remains under your control.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage

Life Currency uses a local database to store your activities, intentions, and reflections.

Your information:

- Is stored on your device.
- Is accessible through the application.
- Is not automatically synchronized with external servers operated by the developer.

### Backups

If you choose to export or back up your data, you control where the resulting files are stored.

Please be aware that files saved to third-party cloud storage services may be subject to those providers' privacy policies.

---

## 5. Analytics and Third-Party Services

Life Currency does not intentionally use:

- Third-party advertising networks.
- Behavioral tracking tools.
- Analytics services that collect personal information.

The application may rely on operating-system services necessary for normal functionality.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored locally until you delete it or remove the application.

You can delete individual records through the application's available features.

Uninstalling Life Currency generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Life Currency is not specifically directed toward children.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 8. Security

Life Currency is designed to keep your personal activity and reflection data on your device.

We recommend keeping your device updated and using available device security features.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised **Last updated** date.
